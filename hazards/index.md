# Hazards

* [Arc radiation](arc-radiation.md) - Welding arcs emit intense visible, ultraviolet, and infrared radiation.
* [Burns](burns.md) - Contact with hot surfaces, sparks, molten material, or corrosive chemicals.
* [Chemical exposure](chemical-exposure.md) - Chemicals may enter the body through inhalation, skin or eye contact, or ingestion.
* [Fall from height](fall-from-height.md) - Falling to a lower level or through an opening.
* [Flying particles](flying-particles.md) - Particles may strike or enter the eyes and face.
* [High noise](high-noise.md) - Excessive sound exposure can damage hearing.
* [Trench collapse](trench-collapse.md) - Soil or excavation walls may collapse and trap or crush workers.
* [Welding fumes](welding-fumes.md) - Inhalation of metal fumes and hazardous gases.
