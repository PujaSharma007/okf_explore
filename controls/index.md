# Preventive Measures

* [Chemical containment and ventilation](chemical-containment-and-ventilation.md) - Use compatible closed-transfer equipment and suitable ventilation where required by the chemical and exposure assessment.
* [Chemical labeling and safety data sheets](chemical-labeling-and-safety-data-sheets.md) - Maintain appropriate labels, accessible safety data sheets, hazard communication, and employee training.
* [Choose quieter equipment](choose-quieter-equipment.md) - Where feasible, substitute quieter equipment or processes for noisy alternatives.
* [Excavation protective systems](excavation-protective-systems.md) - Use properly designed sloping, benching, shoring, or shielding as applicable.
* [Guardrails and suitable covers](guardrails-and-suitable-covers.md) - Install compliant guardrails or covers where required to protect workers from falls.
* [Inspections](inspections.md) - Inspect excavations and protective systems as required, and inspect equipment before use.
* [Local exhaust ventilation](local-exhaust-ventilation.md) - Capture welding fumes near their source and provide adequate ventilation.
* [Machine guards and particle screens](machine-guards-and-particle-screens.md) - Use suitable machine guarding and screens to control contact and flying-particle exposure.
* [Noise monitoring and hearing conservation](noise-monitoring-and-hearing-conservation.md) - Assess noise exposure and implement the applicable hearing conservation measures.
* [Remove hazardous chemical use](remove-hazardous-chemical-use.md) - Eliminate unnecessary chemical use where the process allows.
* [Remove unnecessary work at height](remove-unnecessary-work-at-height.md) - Perform suitable inspections or tasks from ground level when feasible.
* [Training and safe-work procedures](training-and-safe-work-procedures.md) - Train workers on task hazards, safe operating procedures, emergency actions, and applicable controls.
* [Use less hazardous chemicals](use-less-hazardous-chemicals.md) - Evaluate a less hazardous product or process before use.
* [Welding screens](welding-screens.md) - Use suitable noncombustible or flameproof screens to shield nearby people from welding radiation.
* [Work permits and authorization](work-permits-and-authorization.md) - Use the permits and authorizations required by the applicable workplace procedures, including welding authorization where required.
