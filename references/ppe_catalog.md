# PPE Catalog

## hard-hat

- Purpose: Protects the head against specified impact and falling-object hazards.
- Hazards: trench-collapse.
- Limitations: Does not prevent trench collapse or guarantee protection from crushing forces.
- Inspection: Check the shell, suspension, fit, and manufacturer service-life instructions. Remove damaged equipment from service.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.135

## safety-glasses

- Purpose: Protects the eyes against specified flying particles.
- Hazards: flying-particles, chemical-exposure.
- Limitations: Ordinary safety glasses do not provide complete face protection or protect against every chemical splash.
- Inspection: Check lenses, frames, side protection, fit, and markings. Replace damaged or badly scratched equipment.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.133

## welding-helmet

- Purpose: Protects the eyes and face against welding arc radiation and specified welding hazards.
- Hazards: arc-radiation, burns.
- Limitations: The correct filter shade and suitable additional protection are needed. It is not a substitute for ventilation or suitable respiratory protection.
- Inspection: Check the shell, filter, cover lenses, and headgear before use. Replace damaged components.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.252

## respirator

- Purpose: Reduces inhalation exposure when correctly selected for the identified airborne hazard.
- Hazards: welding-fumes, chemical-exposure.
- Limitations: A respirator must match the contaminant and exposure. Required respirator use involves the applicable respiratory protection program, medical evaluation, fit testing, and training. A particulate filter does not automatically protect against gases or vapors.
- Inspection: Follow manufacturer instructions. Check condition, fit, seals, filters, cartridges, and service-life limits before use.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.134

## hearing-protection

- Purpose: Reduces exposure to hazardous workplace noise.
- Hazards: high-noise.
- Limitations: Protection depends on fit and adequate noise reduction. Hearing protection does not eliminate the need for noise assessment and engineering controls.
- Inspection: Check earplugs or earmuffs for damage, cleanliness, fit, and deterioration. Replace as necessary.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.95

## safety-harness

- Purpose: Forms part of a compatible personal fall-arrest system when required and properly connected.
- Hazards: fall-from-height.
- Limitations: A harness alone does not stop a fall. The complete system needs suitable anchorage, connectors, clearance, and a rescue plan.
- Inspection: Inspect before each use and after events that may affect integrity. Remove defective equipment from service and follow manufacturer inspection and replacement instructions.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.140

## chemical-gloves

- Purpose: Protects hands against chemicals that the glove material is suitable for.
- Hazards: chemical-exposure, burns.
- Limitations: No glove material protects against every chemical. Compatibility depends on the chemical, concentration, temperature, contact duration, and glove construction.
- Inspection: Check for holes, tears, degradation, contamination, and loss of integrity. Replace according to the manufacturer's instructions and the chemical safety procedure.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.138

## safety-boots

- Purpose: Protects feet against identified workplace hazards when the footwear is appropriately rated.
- Hazards: trench-collapse, chemical-exposure, burns.
- Limitations: Ordinary safety footwear does not guarantee protection from cave-ins, all chemicals, or every heat exposure.
- Inspection: Check the sole, upper, closures, protective components, and chemical or heat resistance as applicable. Replace damaged or unsuitable footwear.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.136

## PPE selection note

These hazard associations are simplified examples for the learning project. Verify actual PPE requirements against the workplace hazard assessment and relevant manufacturer documentation.
