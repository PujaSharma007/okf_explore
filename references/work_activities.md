# Work Activities

## welding

- Description: Joining or cutting metal using welding processes.
- Typical tasks: Arc welding, gas welding, metal joining, repair welding.
- Hazards: welding-fumes, arc-radiation, burns.
- Sources:
  - OSHA 1910.252: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.252

## grinding

- Description: Removing material or smoothing surfaces using powered abrasive equipment.
- Typical tasks: Surface grinding, deburring, sharpening, weld finishing.
- Hazards: flying-particles, high-noise, burns.
- Sources:
  - OSHA 1910.133: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.133
  - OSHA 1910.95: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.95

## roof-work

- Description: Performing maintenance, installation, or inspection on roofs.
- Typical tasks: Roof inspection, repair, installation, equipment maintenance.
- Hazards: fall-from-height.
- Sources:
  - OSHA 1926.501: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.501

## excavation

- Description: Removing soil to create trenches, pits, or foundations.
- Typical tasks: Trenching, utility installation, foundation digging, soil removal.
- Hazards: trench-collapse, fall-from-height.
- Sources:
  - OSHA 1926.651: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.651
  - OSHA 1926.652: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.652

## chemical-handling

- Description: Transferring, mixing, dispensing, or using workplace chemicals.
- Typical tasks: Pouring chemicals, preparing mixtures, transferring liquids, cleaning.
- Hazards: chemical-exposure, burns.
- Sources:
  - OSHA 1910.1200: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1200
  - OSHA 1910.138: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.138
