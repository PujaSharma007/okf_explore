# Safety Rules

## fall-protection

- Official citation: OSHA 29 CFR 1926.501, Duty to have fall protection.
- URL: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.501
- Summary: Protect workers from falls where the applicable construction requirements call for protection. Depending on the situation, compliant systems may include guardrails, covers, safety nets, or personal fall-arrest systems.
- Applies to: Employers and workers covered by the applicable construction fall-protection requirements.
- PPE: safety-harness.
- Hazards: fall-from-height.

## respiratory-protection

- Official citation: OSHA 29 CFR 1910.134, Respiratory protection.
- URL: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.134
- Summary: When respirators are necessary, employers must establish and maintain the required respiratory protection program. This includes appropriate selection, medical evaluation, fit testing where required, training, and maintenance.
- Applies to: Employers and employees covered by the respiratory protection standard.
- PPE: respirator.
- Hazards: welding-fumes, chemical-exposure.

## hearing-conservation

- Official citation: OSHA 29 CFR 1910.95, Occupational noise exposure.
- URL: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.95
- Summary: Control occupational noise exposure. At or above the 85 dBA eight-hour time-weighted average action level, the standard requires a hearing conservation program. Hearing protectors must be provided and used as specified by the standard.
- Applies to: Employers and employees covered by the general-industry occupational noise standard.
- PPE: hearing-protection.
- Hazards: high-noise.

## head-protection

- Official citation: OSHA 29 CFR 1910.135, Head protection.
- URL: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.135
- Summary: Employees must use appropriate head protection where workplace hazards require it. Protection must meet the applicable requirements and suit the hazard.
- Applies to: Employers and employees covered by the general-industry head-protection standard.
- PPE: hard-hat.
- Hazards: trench-collapse.

## eye-face-protection

- Official citation: OSHA 29 CFR 1910.133, Eye and face protection.
- URL: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.133
- Summary: Provide appropriate eye or face protection when employees are exposed to hazards such as flying particles, molten metal, harmful chemicals, or injurious light radiation.
- Applies to: Employers and employees covered by the general-industry eye and face protection standard.
- PPE: safety-glasses, welding-helmet.
- Hazards: flying-particles, arc-radiation, chemical-exposure, burns.

## excavation-safety

- Official citation: OSHA 29 CFR 1926.651, Specific Excavation Requirements, and 1926.652, Requirements for protective systems.
- URLs:
  - https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.651
  - https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.652
- Summary: Address excavation hazards, including underground utilities, access, inspections, and cave-in protection. Employees in covered excavations must be protected by an adequate protective system unless a specified exception applies.
- Applies to: Employers and employees covered by the construction excavation standards.
- PPE: hard-hat, safety-boots.
- Hazards: trench-collapse.

## Scope note

The citations above are official US OSHA standards. This file is a learning reference, not a complete legal compliance checklist. Verify the applicable industry, jurisdiction, and current requirements before workplace use.
