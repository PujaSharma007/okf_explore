# Preventive Measures

Non-PPE controls should be considered before relying on PPE. Select controls based on the actual hazard assessment and applicable requirements.

## Elimination

### Remove unnecessary work at height
- Measure: Perform suitable inspections or tasks from ground level when feasible.
- Hazards reduced: fall-from-height.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.501

### Remove hazardous chemical use
- Measure: Eliminate unnecessary chemical use where the process allows.
- Hazards reduced: chemical-exposure, burns.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1200

## Substitution

### Use less hazardous chemicals
- Measure: Evaluate a less hazardous product or process before use.
- Hazards reduced: chemical-exposure, burns.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1200

### Choose quieter equipment
- Measure: Where feasible, substitute quieter equipment or processes for noisy alternatives.
- Hazards reduced: high-noise.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.95

## Engineering

### Local exhaust ventilation
- Measure: Capture welding fumes near their source and provide adequate ventilation.
- Hazards reduced: welding-fumes.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.252

### Machine guards and particle screens
- Measure: Use suitable machine guarding and screens to control contact and flying-particle exposure.
- Hazards reduced: flying-particles.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.212

### Guardrails and suitable covers
- Measure: Install compliant guardrails or covers where required to protect workers from falls.
- Hazards reduced: fall-from-height.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.501

### Excavation protective systems
- Measure: Use properly designed sloping, benching, shoring, or shielding as applicable.
- Hazards reduced: trench-collapse.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.652

### Chemical containment and ventilation
- Measure: Use compatible closed-transfer equipment and suitable ventilation where required by the chemical and exposure assessment.
- Hazards reduced: chemical-exposure, burns.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1200

### Welding screens
- Measure: Use suitable noncombustible or flameproof screens to shield nearby people from welding radiation.
- Hazards reduced: arc-radiation.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.252

## Administrative

### Training and safe-work procedures
- Measure: Train workers on task hazards, safe operating procedures, emergency actions, and applicable controls.
- Hazards reduced: welding-fumes, flying-particles, fall-from-height, high-noise, chemical-exposure, trench-collapse, arc-radiation, burns.
- Sources:
  - https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.132
  - https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1200

### Work permits and authorization
- Measure: Use the permits and authorizations required by the applicable workplace procedures, including welding authorization where required.
- Hazards reduced: burns, welding-fumes, arc-radiation.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.252

### Inspections
- Measure: Inspect excavations and protective systems as required, and inspect equipment before use.
- Hazards reduced: trench-collapse, fall-from-height, flying-particles.
- Sources:
  - https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.651
  - https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.212

### Noise monitoring and hearing conservation
- Measure: Assess noise exposure and implement the applicable hearing conservation measures.
- Hazards reduced: high-noise.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.95

### Chemical labeling and safety data sheets
- Measure: Maintain appropriate labels, accessible safety data sheets, hazard communication, and employee training.
- Hazards reduced: chemical-exposure, burns.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1200

## Implementation note

This is a starter list. Actual controls depend on the task, site conditions, exposure assessment, and applicable requirements.
