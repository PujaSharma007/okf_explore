# Limitations

A respirator must match the contaminant and exposure. Required respirator use involves the applicable respiratory protection program, medical evaluation, fit testing, and training. A particulate filter does not automatically protect against gases or vapors.

# Inspection

Follow manufacturer instructions. Check condition, fit, seals, filters, cartridges, and service-life limits before use.
