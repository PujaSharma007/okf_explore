# Limitations

No glove material protects against every chemical. Compatibility depends on the chemical, concentration, temperature, contact duration, and glove construction.

# Inspection

Check for holes, tears, degradation, contamination, and loss of integrity. Replace according to the manufacturer's instructions and the chemical safety procedure.
