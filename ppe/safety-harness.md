# Limitations

A harness alone does not stop a fall. The complete system needs suitable anchorage, connectors, clearance, and a rescue plan.

# Inspection

Inspect before each use and after events that may affect integrity. Remove defective equipment from service and follow manufacturer inspection and replacement instructions.
