# Limitations

Ordinary safety footwear does not guarantee protection from cave-ins, all chemicals, or every heat exposure.

# Inspection

Check the sole, upper, closures, protective components, and chemical or heat resistance as applicable. Replace damaged or unsuitable footwear.
