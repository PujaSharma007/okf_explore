# PPE

* [Chemical gloves](chemical-gloves.md) - Protects hands against chemicals that the glove material is suitable for.
* [Hard hat](hard-hat.md) - Protects the head against specified impact and falling-object hazards.
* [Hearing protection](hearing-protection.md) - Reduces exposure to hazardous workplace noise.
* [Respirator](respirator.md) - Reduces inhalation exposure when correctly selected for the identified airborne hazard.
* [Safety boots](safety-boots.md) - Protects feet against identified workplace hazards when the footwear is appropriately rated.
* [Safety glasses](safety-glasses.md) - Protects the eyes against specified flying particles.
* [Safety harness](safety-harness.md) - Forms part of a compatible personal fall-arrest system when required and properly connected.
* [Welding helmet](welding-helmet.md) - Protects the eyes and face against welding arc radiation and specified welding hazards.
