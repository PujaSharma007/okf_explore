Protect workers from falls where the applicable construction requirements call for protection. Depending on the situation, compliant systems may include guardrails, covers, safety nets, or personal fall-arrest systems.[^safety-rules]

# Official citation

OSHA 29 CFR 1926.501, Duty to have fall protection.

# Applies to

Employers and workers covered by the applicable construction fall-protection requirements.
