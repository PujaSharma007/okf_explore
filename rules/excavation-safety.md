Address excavation hazards, including underground utilities, access, inspections, and cave-in protection. Employees in covered excavations must be protected by an adequate protective system unless a specified exception applies.[^safety-rules]

# Official citation

OSHA 29 CFR 1926.651, Specific Excavation Requirements, and 1926.652, Requirements for protective systems.

# Applies to

Employers and employees covered by the construction excavation standards.
