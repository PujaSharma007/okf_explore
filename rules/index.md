# Rules

* [Excavation safety](excavation-safety.md) - Address excavation hazards, including underground utilities, access, inspections, and cave-in protection. Employees in covered excavations must be protected by an adequate protective system unless a specified exception applies.
* [Eye and face protection](eye-face-protection.md) - Provide appropriate eye or face protection when employees are exposed to hazards such as flying particles, molten metal, harmful chemicals, or injurious light radiation.
* [Fall protection](fall-protection.md) - Protect workers from falls where the applicable construction requirements call for protection. Depending on the situation, compliant systems may include guardrails, covers, safety nets, or personal fall-arrest systems.
* [Head protection](head-protection.md) - Employees must use appropriate head protection where workplace hazards require it. Protection must meet the applicable requirements and suit the hazard.
* [Hearing conservation](hearing-conservation.md) - Control occupational noise exposure. At or above the 85 dBA eight-hour time-weighted average action level, the standard requires a hearing conservation program. Hearing protectors must be provided and used as specified by the standard.
* [Respiratory protection](respiratory-protection.md) - When respirators are necessary, employers must establish and maintain the required respiratory protection program. This includes appropriate selection, medical evaluation, fit testing where required, training, and maintenance.
