Control occupational noise exposure. At or above the 85 dBA eight-hour time-weighted average action level, the standard requires a hearing conservation program. Hearing protectors must be provided and used as specified by the standard.[^safety-rules]

# Official citation

OSHA 29 CFR 1910.95, Occupational noise exposure.

# Applies to

Employers and employees covered by the general-industry occupational noise standard.
